---

# Talk Overview


Using a Chinese conceptualization of social capital, informal interviews, and two decades of official data, Professor Joshua Eisenman examines how and why the Communist Party of China (CPC) builds relationships with African political elites.


The International Department of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (ID-CPC) has become a primary party organization responsible for strengthening China's relational power with political partners across Africa, regardless of their ideological backgrounds.


The talk explores how the ID-CPC has expanded and deepened relationships with African political elites, maintained these connections virtually during the COVID-19 pandemic, and resumed in-person exchanges after China's travel restrictions were lifted.


---

# China-Africa Political Engagement


Professor Eisenman discusses how the ID-CPC engages African counterparts through initiatives including:

- Bilateral and multilateral "host diplomacy"
- Cadre training programs
- Exchanges focused on governance methods


These activities provide opportunities for sharing Chinese governance experiences while strengthening political relationships and mutual engagement.

# Selected Publications


Professor Eisenman is the author of:

*China’s Relations with Africa: A New Era of Strategic Engagement*  
(Columbia University Press, 2023)

with David H. Shinn.


The book examines how China builds relationships with African countries to advance its broader geostrategic goals.


His previous book:

*China and Africa: A Century of Engagement*

was named one of the "Best International Relations Books of 2012" by *Foreign Affairs*.


His research on China-Africa political relations has appeared in journals including:

- *Third World Quarterly*
- *Journal of Contemporary China*


His work has also been cited by publications including *The New York Times* and *The Economist*.
