# Speaker


## Brian Linden


Brian Linden is the Founder of Linden Centre, an innovative heritage tourism and community development project in China.


Born and raised in Chicago, Brian began working in Beijing in 1984, where he played a leading role in one of the first films since 1949 to feature a foreign leading actor.


He later worked for CBS News in Beijing, participating in interviews with many Chinese leaders, including Deng Xiaoping.


Brian completed graduate studies at the University of Illinois, Hopkins-Nanjing Center, and Stanford University.


---

# Professional Journey


Brian's early career focused on international education development and investment. He worked in more than 75 countries before returning to China in 2004 with his wife and two sons.


Together, they began preserving and redeveloping a nationally protected historical structure, creating what became the Linden Centre.


The project represents an innovative approach to tourism development by combining heritage preservation, local participation, and sustainable community growth.


---

# Talk Overview


In this talk, Brian Linden will discuss how the Linden Centre model incorporates local villagers into the planning and management process while using existing historic structures to revitalize communities.


He will share insights into:

- Sustainable tourism development
- Heritage preservation
- Community-based planning
- Collaboration with local governments
- Creating partnerships between international organizations and regional communities


The Linden Centre model demonstrates how tourism can move beyond traditional development approaches by creating partnerships that benefit local communities and preserve cultural heritage.


---

# Building Sustainable Communities


Today, the Linden Centre operates seven sites, each focused on preserving historic buildings while involving local villagers in creating and managing heritage hotels.


The model has received recognition from organizations and leaders including the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the US Secretary of State, and scholars and media around the world.


Brian will also discuss his evolving perspectives on China and international collaboration, drawing from his nearly four decades of experience working between cultures.
