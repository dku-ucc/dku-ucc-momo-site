# Talk Overview


The trapped ion system is one of the leading physical platforms for realizing practical quantum computers and quantum simulators.


Recently, the vibrational degrees of freedom of trapped ions have attracted increasing attention for quantum information processing using continuous variables.


In particular, multiple vibrational modes can be configured as bosonic networks capable of performing boson sampling and demonstrating quantum advantages.


Compared with photonic systems, phononic systems provide unique advantages: phonon number states can be deterministically prepared and detected, and collective modes can maintain strong control over quantum states.


---

# Scalable Phononic Networks


In this lecture, Professor Kim will introduce the development of a programmable phononic network consisting of up to four vibrational modes.


The network enables:

- Arbitrary programming of connections between modes
- Beam-splitting operations between mode pairs through ion-qubit coupling
- Deterministic preparation and detection of quantum states


The research demonstrates a new pathway for scaling phononic networks and advancing quantum information processing beyond the limitations of classical computing and other quantum systems.
