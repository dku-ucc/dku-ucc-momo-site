# Event Recap: A Conversation with Luo Tong: An Award-winning Documentary Filmmaker


![Event Poster](/images/events/luo-tong-2024/poster.png)


The Duke Kunshan University Colloquium Committee (UCC) and the Center for the Study of Contemporary China (CSCC) Digital Technology and Society Cluster hosted a conversation with award-winning documentary filmmaker Luo Tong.


The event brought together students, faculty, and community members to explore documentary filmmaking, storytelling, and the creative process behind bringing real-world stories to audiences.

Through her filmmaking journey, Luo Tong shared reflections on storytelling, empathy, and the relationship between filmmakers and the communities they document.


## Reflections on Storytelling


The conversation explored the relationship between filmmaking, storytelling, empathy, and technology.

Several reflections from the discussion highlighted the importance of emotional connection, passion, and responsible storytelling in documentary filmmaking.


![Reflections from the Conversation](/images/events/luo-tong-2024/quotes.png)


The conversation highlighted several themes central to documentary filmmaking:

- storytelling as a way to preserve human experiences;
- the importance of empathy and emotional connection;
- the role of technology in documenting cultural heritage;
- passion and commitment in creative practice.


## About the Speaker


Luo Tong is a documentary filmmaker and producer with extensive experience in independent film production.

Her works include *A Farewell Song*, *The Poseidon Project*, *A City Sets Sail*, and *The Six*, an award-winning documentary about Chinese survivors of RMS Titanic.
