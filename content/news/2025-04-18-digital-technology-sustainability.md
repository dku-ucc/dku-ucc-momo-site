# Digital Technology for Sustainability Symposium and Finance Forum 2025


Duke Kunshan University will host the Digital Technology for Sustainability Symposium and Finance Forum 2025 on April 18, 2025.

This event brings together scholars, industry experts, and students to explore how digital technology can contribute to sustainability and responsible innovation.

April 18, 2025


**Location**

- Industry partners
