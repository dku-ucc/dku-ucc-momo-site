# United in Innovation: Pioneering Technologies for a Better Tomorrow


![Event Poster](/images/events/united-in-innovation-2024/poster.png)


The Duke Kunshan University Colloquium Committee (UCC) presents:

**"United in Innovation: Pioneering Technologies for a Better Tomorrow."**


Hosted by the University Colloquium Committee at Duke Kunshan University (DKU), and co-hosted by the Data Science Research Center (DSRC), DKU Finance and Computer Science Club, and Microsoft Asia Pacific Research and Development (R&D) Group, this colloquium brings together technology pioneers, researchers, and students to explore the future of innovation.


In a world facing unprecedented challenges, technological innovation has become a catalyst for change. This event aims to foster university-industry collaboration and explore how emerging technologies can address global challenges, improve quality of life, and contribute to a sustainable future.

# Event Theme


The colloquium focuses on collaborative innovation across emerging technologies, including:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Web3
- Extended Reality (XR)
- Cloud Computing
- Sustainable economic initiatives


Through interactive sessions and discussions, participants will explore how technological advancement can be connected with social impact and human development.

# Hosts and Organizers


## University Colloquium Committee (UCC)

Established in 2017, DSRC focuses on developing transformative algorithms, tools, and methodologies for data analytics across science, engineering, medicine, policy, and business.
