# Gender, Robots, and Global Commons:
# Interdisciplinary Perspectives on Computational Social Science


![Forum Poster](/images/events/computational-social-science-2025/poster.png)


The Duke Kunshan University Colloquium Committee (UCC), together with the Center for the Study of Contemporary China (CSCC), the Division of Social Sciences, and the SocioTech Future Lab, will host the **Interdisciplinary Forum on Computational Social Science**.


Under the theme of **"Gender, Robots, and Global Commons: Cross-Disciplinary Perspectives on Ethics, Equity, and Socio-Technical Futures,"** this forum brings together scholars and students from diverse disciplines to examine how computational technologies reshape society.


The forum will explore emerging questions around artificial intelligence, social equity, digital governance, and responsible technological development.

# About the Forum


As intelligent technologies rapidly transform society, computational social science provides new ways to understand the relationship between technology and human communities.


This interdisciplinary forum creates a platform for dialogue among scholars, practitioners, and students to explore:

- Ethical challenges in emerging technologies
- Gender and inclusion in computational systems
- Responsible AI governance
- The future of socio-technical innovation


By bringing together perspectives from computer science, social science, humanities, and policy studies, the forum aims to foster more inclusive and sustainable approaches to technological development.
