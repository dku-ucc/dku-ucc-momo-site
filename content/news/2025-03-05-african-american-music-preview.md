# Muddy Waters to Urban Jungles: A Discourse on African American Music from Blues to Rock to Hip-Hop


![Event Poster](/images/events/african-american-music-2025/poster.png)


The Duke Kunshan University Colloquium Committee (UCC) will host **"Muddy Waters to Urban Jungles: A Discourse on African American Music from Blues to Rock to Hip-Hop."**


This special event brings together three major figures in music history to explore the evolution of African American music—from the raw emotion of blues, through the rebellious energy of rock, and ultimately to the groundbreaking artistry of hip-hop.


Through live demonstrations, storytelling, and rare record collections, the event will examine how different generations and genres of Black music have influenced one another and shaped global cultural landscapes.

# Speakers


## Brian Offenther / DJ BO

Lecturer, DJ, and Event Producer


Brian Offenther (DJ BO) is a Shanghai-based DJ and event producer who has collaborated with universities and cultural institutions across China and internationally.

His work explores the connections between music, history, and culture through performances, lectures, and interdisciplinary events.


## Sugar Blue

Grammy Award-winning Harmonica Virtuoso


Sugar Blue is a Grammy Award-winning harmonica player, composer, and bandleader renowned for his contributions to blues and rock music.

He is widely recognized for his work on The Rolling Stones' iconic track "Miss You" and collaborations with artists including Bob Dylan, Willie Dixon, Prince, and DMC of Run-DMC.


## Afrika Islam

Pioneering Hip-Hop DJ/Producer


Afrika Islam is one of hip-hop’s pioneering DJs and producers, known for his long-term collaborations with Ice-T and contributions to hip-hop culture internationally.

He is recognized as one of the first hip-hop DJs in Asia and has introduced rare historical music collections through educational programs and exhibitions.

# Event Overview


## Blues, the "Pop" of Rock


A forum interview with Sugar Blue will explore how blues became the foundation of rock music and influenced generations of musicians.


## Hip-Hop, a Step-Child of Rock


Afrika Islam will share rare records and stories from hip-hop history, highlighting the connections between hip-hop, jazz, blues, and rock through African American cultural history.


By bridging generations and genres, this event offers a unique perspective on the interconnected legacy of Black music—from Muddy Waters to today's urban jungles.
