# Event Recap: Muddy Waters to Urban Jungles: A Discourse on African American Music


![Event Poster](/images/events/african-american-music-2025/poster.png)


On March 17, from 5:30 to 7:00 P.M., the DKU Performance Café came alive with rhythm and reflection during **"Muddy Waters to Urban Jungles: A Discourse on African American Music."**


Hosted by the Duke Kunshan University Colloquium Committee (UCC) and co-hosted by the CSCC Digital Technology and Society Cluster, the event featured Grammy-winning blues legend Sugar Blue, hip-hop pioneer Afrika Islam, and moderator DJ BO (Brian Offenther).


Through live demonstrations, storytelling, and rare vinyl unboxing, the speakers traced the powerful lineage of African American music—from the deep roots of blues, through rock, to the revolutionary soundscape of hip-hop—highlighting its global impact and enduring cultural legacy.


---

# Event Highlights


![Performance Highlight](/images/events/african-american-music-2025/performance.png)


The evening explored two interconnected chapters of African American music history.


## Blues, the "Pop" of Rock


Sugar Blue shared his experiences as a Grammy-winning harmonica virtuoso and reflected on the influence of blues on rock music and popular culture.


## Hip-Hop, a Step-Child of Rock


Afrika Islam introduced audiences to the history of hip-hop through rare record collections and stories from the genre’s early development.


DJ BO guided the conversation by connecting musical evolution across generations, from Muddy Waters’ blues traditions to contemporary hip-hop culture.
